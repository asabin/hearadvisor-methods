# Results

## Component Metrics

This procedure has produced complete metrics for the **109 hearing devices** currently listed on hearadvisor.com, each scored on our 5-point scales. The speech metrics below reflect the blended Speech Perception Benefit score introduced in August 2026 (average of the \(\Delta\)HASPIv2 and listener-rated components, each bounded below at 1.0).

*Table 4. Average Component Scores Across 109 Devices.* All metrics are on a scale from 0--5 points where higher is better. Music streaming scores are reported only for devices with streaming capability (n=68).

| | Speech Benefit (Quiet/Mod) | Speech Benefit (Noisy) | Own Voice Not Boomy | Does Not Squeal | Streaming Music Quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Initial Fits** Avg (s.d.) | 2.9 (1.1) | 1.6 (0.7) | 2.6 (1.0) | 4.6 (0.7) | 3.4 (0.8) |
| **Tuned Fits** Avg (s.d.) | 3.3 (1.1) | 1.8 (0.8) | 2.4 (1.0) | 4.3 (0.9) | 3.4 (0.7) |

## SoundScore Results

As of August 2026, across the **109 listed devices**:

- Average SoundScore: **3.8** (s.d. 0.6)
- Expert Choice threshold: **4.0** (see [Expert Choice Award](metrics/soundscore.md#expert-choice-award))
- Devices meeting threshold: **48** (44% of tested products)

*Table 5. SoundGrade.* Mapping of SoundScore to SoundGrade. Data as of August 2026, following the Speech Perception Benefit recompute.

| SoundScore | SoundGrade | N Devices |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0--5.0 (inclusive) | A | 48 (44%) |
| 3.0--&lt;4.0 | B | 52 (48%) |
| 2.0--&lt;3.0 | C | 9 (8%) |
| 1.0--&lt;2.0 | D | 0 (0%) |
| 0.0--&lt;1.0 | F | 0 (0%) |

SoundGrades are assigned from SoundScores rounded to one decimal using half-up rounding. The 1.0 floor on both speech components means no tested device now scores below 2.0 overall.

!!! tip "Full Results"
    Detailed per-device results, recordings, and comparisons are available at [hearadvisor.com](https://hearadvisor.com). This methodology documentation describes *how* the scores are generated; the website presents the scores themselves.
